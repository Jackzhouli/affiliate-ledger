Affiliate Ledger Privacy Policy

How Affiliate Ledger accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google Ads campaign data.

Privacy Policy

Affiliate Ledger is a personal tool operated by its owner for affiliate marketing campaign organization and Google Ads keyword research.

Data We Access

When you connect the tool to Google Ads, it may access the following data from the linked Google Ads account:

  • Campaign and ad group information (names, status, budgets, bids)
  • Keyword data, including match types and performance metrics
  • Ad creative and sitelink information used for campaign management
  • Account-level settings needed to execute requested changes

How Data Is Used

The data is used only to:

  • Research and evaluate keywords for affiliate marketing campaigns
  • Review available advertising metrics
  • Organize and update campaign information based on the owner's instructions

We do not use Google Ads data for any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

Data Storage and Sharing

Campaign and keyword information processed by this tool is stored in the owner's private Google Sheets workspace. No Google Ads data is stored on this website, and it is not shared with other users or third parties beyond the Google services described here.

Contact

If you have questions about this tool or how your data is handled, email the site owner at contact@opportunitiessite.com.
