About This Tool

What Affiliate Ledger Is For

Affiliate Ledger is a personal software tool used by its owner to organize affiliate marketing campaigns and review Google Ads keyword and campaign data.

The purpose of Affiliate Ledger is to:

  • Research and evaluate keywords for affiliate marketing campaigns
  • Review available Google Ads advertising metrics (such as impressions, clicks, and cost-related performance where available)
  • Organize campaign, ad group, and keyword information for the owner's own affiliate marketing work
  • Support limited campaign updates based on the owner's instructions

Affiliate Ledger is not a public social network, not a consumer login portal, and not an AI image-generation product. It does not create or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII), and Google user data accessed through Google APIs is not used for that purpose.

How It Works

Affiliate Ledger connects to the Google Ads API (with the account owner's authorization) to read campaign and keyword information needed for research and organization. Access to Google Ads data is limited to the purposes described on this page and in the Affiliate Ledger Privacy Policy.

This website explains the tool for users and reviewers. Browsing this homepage and the policy pages does not require signing in.

Google API Use & Compliance

Affiliate Ledger uses Google Ads API data only to support keyword research and campaign analysis for the owner's affiliate marketing activities. Use of Google APIs complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and related Google policies. Google user data is not sold, and it is not used to train unrelated AI models or to generate prohibited content.
