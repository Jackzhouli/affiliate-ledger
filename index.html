About This Tool

Affiliate Ledger is a personal tool for organizing affiliate marketing campaigns and reviewing Google Ads keyword and campaign data.

The tool is used by its owner to research keywords, review available advertising metrics, and organize campaign information for affiliate marketing activities.

How It Works

Affiliate Ledger uses Google Ads API data to support keyword research and campaign analysis. Access to Google Ads data is limited to the purposes described here and in the Privacy Policy.
