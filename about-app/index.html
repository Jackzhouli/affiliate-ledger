Affiliate Ledger

Affiliate Ledger is a personal tool for organizing affiliate marketing campaigns and reviewing Google Ads keyword and campaign data.

About This Tool

The purpose of Affiliate Ledger is to help its owner research keywords, review available advertising metrics, and organize campaign information for affiliate marketing activities.

How It Works

Affiliate Ledger uses Google Ads API data to support keyword research and campaign analysis. Access to Google Ads data is limited to the purposes described here and in the Privacy Policy.

Google user data

Affiliate Ledger’s use of information received from Google APIs, including the Google Ads API, adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Ads data is not sold and is not shared with other users.
